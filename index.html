01 Our story

More than a kava bar.

Out front the stucco is a light blush pink, under a dark awning for Chameleon Kava, coffee, and the drive-thru. The windows say kava and vape. It is a sober lounge and a smoke shop.

Inside, the ceiling is dark and the string lights stay on. There is a long wooden bar — the owner refinished the tops herself — with green stools and pink pendants, a green sofa, and a wall of neon and greenery. Two Simonis pool tables, a karaoke system, and a game lounge. A brighter back room is for arts and crafts, including squishy making, when parents want to sit.

Chameleon is locally owned, a female-owned passion project. It was rebranded from The Joint Kava Lounge, and the product was improved with the name. Weekdays open at 7 AM. Monday, Friday, and Saturday run until 2 AM.
