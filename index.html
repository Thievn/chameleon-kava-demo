01 The lounge

Come hang. Stay as long as you like.

Chameleon is a locally owned, female-owned lounge in Cocoa. Order noble kava, kratom, or craft coffee, or swing through the drive-thru for smoke and vape. There is no alcohol.

Play on two Simonis pool tables, sing when karaoke is up, or settle into a game. Families are welcome. Kids can make squishies in the quiet crafts corner while you sit. Pets can come in too.

We used to be The Joint Kava Lounge. The name changed, and the drinks did too. Weekdays open at 7 AM. Monday, Friday, and Saturday run until 2 AM.
